Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

WillThisFit.me is run by Ashley Keeler (UK). This page explains what personal data the site handles and why. Contact: ashleyjkeeler@gmail.com.

Using the size tools

The converter, measurement matcher and size charts work entirely in your browser. Measurements you type in are not sent to us or stored anywhere. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of our own.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the site. These are kept in your browser only for the current visit, so we can show who is signed in and attach your email to corrections you submit. We do not keep a user database. Google's handling of your data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Submitting corrections

When you submit a correction or new size data, we store what you send (the brand, category, details and, if provided, your email address) in a private Airtable database so we can review it. We use your email only to follow up about that submission. You can ask us to delete a submission at any time.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

Sharing

We never sell or share your data with brands, retailers or advertisers.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Email us and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
