Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

WillThisFit.me is run by Ashley Keeler (UK). This page explains what personal data the site handles and why. Contact: ashleyjkeeler@gmail.com.

Using the size tools

The converter, measurement matcher and size charts work entirely in your browser. Measurements you type in are not sent to us or stored anywhere. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of our own.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture with the site. Our server checks the sign-in with Google each time you save something. We keep your Google account ID, name and email address so your wardrobe and kudos stay with your account. We never show your name or email to other users. Google's handling of your data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Your wardrobe, measurements and kudos

When you're signed in, we store what you add: the clothes in your wardrobe (brand, size, how they fit, garment measurements, roughly when and where you bought them, and any notes), the body measurements you choose to enter in Settings, and the kudos you've earned. We use this to work out your size at other brands and to show you your wardrobe.

Fit reports from many people are combined into anonymous totals, such as "62% say this size runs small". These are shown only once at least 3 people have reported the same brand and size, and never with names. Your body measurements are never shown to anyone or included in those totals.

The leaderboard is opt-in. If you join it, other people see only the display name you choose and your kudos.

You can remove any item at any time, and you can delete your whole account in Wardrobe → Settings. That permanently deletes your wardrobe, measurements and kudos straight away. This data is stored with Supabase in London.

Submitting corrections

When you submit a correction or new size data, we store what you send (the brand, category, details and, if provided, your email address) in a private Airtable database so we can review it. We use your email only to follow up about that submission. You can ask us to delete a submission at any time.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. Our database is run by Supabase (London, UK). Corrections sent through the Contribute form are stored in Airtable.

Sharing

We never sell or share your data with brands, retailers or advertisers.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. You can delete your account yourself in the app, or email us and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
